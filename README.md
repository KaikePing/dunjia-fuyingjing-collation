# 景祐遁甲符应经 · 校订完整版

《景祐遁甲符应经》是北宋仁宗景祐年间官修的遁甲书。网上流传的录文以 [ctext（中国哲学书电子化计划）](https://ctext.org/) 本为主，只有序、卷上、卷中，而且 OCR 错误多，原书空框也被录成了「口」。

本仓库以 ctext 录文为起点，由 Claude Opus 5.5 逐页对照两种古本的书影，重新识读全部文字，再把两本合校，补齐了 ctext 本没有的卷下、下部三卷和王巽后序：

- **三卷本**：宛委别藏本（阮元进呈旧钞本），作上部的底本；
- **明本**：美国国会图书馆藏明末朱丝栏钞本，作上部的校本，也是下部和后序的唯一底本。

成品有两版：[`遁甲符应经_校订完整版_简体.txt`](遁甲符应经_校订完整版_简体.txt)、[`遁甲符应经_校订完整版_繁体.txt`](遁甲符应经_校订完整版_繁体.txt)。校注用〔校：……〕夹在正文里，体例写在文前。逐页精录稿、合校稿和工作记录在 [`遁甲符应经_补缺/`](遁甲符应经_补缺/) 下，见其中的 `校勘记录.md`。

# Jingyou Dunjia Fuying Jing — Collated Complete Edition

The *Jingyou Dunjia Fuying Jing* (景祐遁甲符應經) is a Dunjia divination manual compiled by imperial order in the Jingyou era (1034–1038) of the Northern Song. The text circulating online comes mostly from [ctext (Chinese Text Project)](https://ctext.org/). It covers only the preface and the first two juan, has many OCR errors, and renders the blank boxes of the original (lacunae) as the character 口.

Starting from the ctext transcription, this repository re-reads the whole text page by page against scans of two early editions, with every page transcribed by Claude Claude Opus 5.5. It then collates the two editions and restores everything ctext lacks: the third juan, the three-juan "lower part" (下部), and Wang Xun's postface. The two editions are:

- **Three-juan edition**: from the *Wanwei biecang* collection (a Qing manuscript copy). It serves as the base text for the upper part.
- **Ming edition**: a late-Ming manuscript held by the Library of Congress. It is the collating witness for the upper part and the only source for the lower part and the postface.

The result comes in Simplified and Traditional Chinese (the two `.txt` files above). Editorial notes appear inline as 〔校：……〕, and the editorial conventions are explained at the head of each file. Page-by-page transcriptions, collation drafts and the working log are in [`遁甲符应经_补缺/`](遁甲符应经_补缺/); see `校勘记录.md` there.
